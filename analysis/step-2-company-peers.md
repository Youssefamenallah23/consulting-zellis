## Traps found while verifying (log under issue-tree Branch D)

1. **The dormant-holdco trap**: "IRIS Software Group Limited" (06295385) is SIC 99999 *dormant*. The trading entity is **IRIS Software Limited** (03196054). Pulling accounts from the wrong entity would have produced a null dataset.
2. **Holdco ≠ no data**: OneAdvanced Group (05965280) is SIC 64209 "holding company" but still files full **consolidated** group accounts — SIC code alone doesn't tell you what's in the filing.
3. **Amended accounts**: OneAdvanced filed AAMD (amended accounts) for FY2023 — always check for restatements before trusting a number.
4. **Name changes**: Zellis UK was formerly NorthgateArinso UK → Northgate HR → Rebus HR; OneAdvanced was Advanced Computer Software plc. Search filings under *current* name, but read history under old names.
5. **Acquisition-structure vocabulary**: Topco / Midco / Bidco / Holdco entities are LBO ownership layers — shell companies with no operations. The operating company (OpCo) holds the employees and revenue.
