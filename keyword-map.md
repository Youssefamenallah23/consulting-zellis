- **CV one-liner:** "Built a rightsizing diagnostic of a PE-backed UK company vs. sector peers from public filings — sized £X m EBITDA value at stake across three scenarios; deck to a simulated Investment Committee."
- **Interview story:** frame it as a 6-step engagement (frame → scope → data → analysis → model → recommendation), exactly the "Humatica Way" arc of hypothesis-driven work.
- **The German note (Munich):** project doesn't prove language — keep the bilingual claim separate and prepared.
